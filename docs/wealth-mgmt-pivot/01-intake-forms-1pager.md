## 1 · Digital intake form with CRM auto-sync

**Problem.** Paper or PDFs that get re-typed. Partial fields slip
through. Compliance can't audit a workflow that lives in a filing
cabinet. The ops manager spends 20-30 minutes per new client just on
data entry.

**What we build.** Web form with conditional logic (e.g., joint vs
individual triggers different beneficiary fields), document upload
(driver's license, trust documents, prior statements), and e-signature.
On submit, the data populates Redtail / Wealthbox / Salesforce FSC
automatically — contact record, financial profile, household
relationships, all in one pass.

**Outcome.** **30–45 minutes saved per onboarding.** Compliance gets
a clean audit trail. The ops manager gets their afternoon back.

## 2 · Custodian-of-record automation

**Problem.** Schwab, Fidelity, TD Ameritrade (now Schwab), Pershing —
each has its own paperwork: account application, ACH authorization,
beneficiary designation, IPS attachment. Most firms still send these
one at a time through DocuSign or — worse — print, sign, scan, email.

**What we build.** Pre-fill the custodian forms from the intake-form
data (no re-typing), bulk-package them into a single signing flow,
auto-archive the signed PDFs into the CRM client folder. Custodian
delivery logs are captured for compliance.

**Outcome.** **15–25 minutes saved per new account.** Faster funding,
fewer NIGO ("not in good order") rejects, and an audit-ready document
trail.

## 3 · Risk-tolerance + Investment Policy Statement drafting

**Problem.** Risk-tolerance questionnaires get scored manually on a
calculator. The IPS is drafted from a template by hand for every new
client. Founding partner spends 60-90 minutes per client on a workflow
that could run on rails.

**What we build.** Scored online questionnaire (10-15 questions,
configurable to your firm's risk methodology). Output feeds an IPS
draft using your firm's template — standard sections (objectives,
constraints, allocation, rebalancing policy) populate from the
questionnaire response. Founding partner reviews, edits, and ships in
15 minutes instead of 90.

**Outcome.** **60–90 minutes saved per client.** Consistent IPS
quality across the partner pool. Partial-question detection prevents
incomplete intakes from reaching the CIO.
