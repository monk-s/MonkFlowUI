## DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT

This Data Processing Agreement ("**DPA**") is entered into between:

**Customer:** ___________________________________________ ("Customer"),
the firm engaging MonkFlow to build software systems on its behalf,

**and**

**Processor:** **MonkFlow** (operated by Nathan Linder, sole
proprietor, doing business as MonkFlow) ("**MonkFlow**" or
"**Processor**"),

each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties.**"

**Effective Date:** _____________________

---

### 1. Purpose

This DPA governs the processing of Customer Data (as defined below)
by MonkFlow in the course of performing services pursuant to the
parties' Engagement Letter or master services agreement (the
"**Agreement**").

### 2. Definitions

- **"Customer Data"** means any data submitted to MonkFlow on behalf
  of Customer in connection with the services, including end-client
  personally identifiable information (PII), financial data,
  account data, and ID documents.
- **"Sub-processor"** means any third party engaged by MonkFlow that
  processes Customer Data.
- **"Personal Data"** has the meaning given in applicable data-
  protection laws (e.g., GLBA, state privacy laws applicable to
  financial advisory firms).

### 3. Roles

Customer is the **data controller**. MonkFlow is the **data
processor** acting on Customer's documented instructions.

### 4. Scope of processing

MonkFlow will process Customer Data only:
(a) on Customer's documented instructions (the Agreement and this DPA);
(b) for the purposes of performing the services; and
(c) for as long as necessary to perform the services or as required
    by law.

### 5. Confidentiality

MonkFlow shall ensure that any person authorized to process Customer
Data is bound by appropriate confidentiality obligations.

### 6. Security measures

MonkFlow shall implement and maintain the technical and organizational
measures described in MonkFlow's Security Overview (attached or
delivered separately, as updated from time to time). At minimum:

- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) for all data transmission
- Encryption at rest (AES-256) for any persistent storage
- Two-factor authentication on all administrative access
- Logging of administrative actions, retained 90 days minimum
- Access restricted to personnel with a documented need to know

### 7. Sub-processors

MonkFlow uses the sub-processors listed in the Security Overview.
MonkFlow will provide Customer with at least 30 days' notice of any
new sub-processor or material change in sub-processor scope. Customer
may object on reasonable grounds; if the objection cannot be resolved,
either party may terminate the Agreement.

### 8. Data-subject rights

MonkFlow shall, to the extent legally permitted, promptly notify
Customer of any request from a data subject seeking to exercise their
rights (access, deletion, correction, portability) and shall assist
Customer in responding.

### 9. Personal-data breach

MonkFlow shall notify Customer of a confirmed breach affecting
Customer Data without undue delay and in any event within
**twenty-four (24) hours** of becoming aware. Notification will
include, to the extent known: nature of the breach, categories of
data affected, approximate number of records, likely consequences,
and remediation measures taken.

### 10. Audits

Customer (or its authorized auditor) may, upon at least 30 days'
written notice, audit MonkFlow's compliance with this DPA, no more
than once per year, during business hours, and subject to
confidentiality obligations. Audit costs are borne by Customer
unless the audit reveals a material breach.

### 11. Return or deletion of data

Upon termination of the Agreement or upon Customer's written
request, MonkFlow shall, at Customer's option, return or delete all
Customer Data within 30 days, except as required by law to retain.
A written certification of deletion will be provided on request.

### 12. International transfers

MonkFlow primarily processes Customer Data within the United States.
Where sub-processors operate outside the United States (see
Security Overview), MonkFlow ensures appropriate safeguards are in
place under applicable law.

### 13. Liability

The liability provisions of the Agreement apply to this DPA. Nothing
in this DPA expands MonkFlow's liability beyond the limits set in the
Agreement.

### 14. Term and termination

This DPA is effective as of the Effective Date and continues for the
duration of the Agreement plus any post-termination period required
to return or delete Customer Data.

### 15. Governing law

This DPA is governed by the laws of the State of Texas, without
regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. Disputes shall be resolved
in the state or federal courts located in Dallas County, Texas.
