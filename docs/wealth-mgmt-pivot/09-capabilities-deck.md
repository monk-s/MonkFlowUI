## WHO

**MonkFlow** is a founder-led dev shop that builds the custom infrastructure

## WHAT

Four buckets. Everything we ship sits in one of them.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Forms** | Digital new-client intake with conditional logic, document upload, e-signature. Custodian-of-record paperwork pre-filled + bulk-signed. |
| **Portals** | Branded secure client portals — document vaults, signed-agreement archives, quarterly review prep. |
| **Automations** | CRM workflow automation: reminder sequences, household-relationship sync, risk-tolerance scoring, IPS draft generation. |
| **Deployments** | Founder-led implementation, training, and ongoing care. We build it, deploy it, and pick up the phone after. |

We **extend** the CRM. We don't replace it, and we don't compete with it.

## WHO FOR

Independent advisor firms (RIAs):

- **State-registered**, $25M–$100M AUM, 2–10 advisors
- On **Redtail, Wealthbox, or Salesforce Financial Services Cloud**
- Custodied at Schwab, Fidelity, or Pershing
- Feeling the new-client-onboarding paperwork bottleneck

Not a fit (we'll say so): SEC-registered $100M+ firms with heavy vendor-
questionnaire processes, hybrid broker-dealers, firms with no CRM yet.
