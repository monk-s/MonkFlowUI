3 Highest-ROI Automations
for E-Commerce Brands

Brands doing $500K–$10M a year bleed 15+ hours a week on manual order processing, inventory sync between Shopify and the warehouse, and one-off customer service copy-paste. These three builds wipe that out.

1
Order-to-fulfillment pipeline
Saves 15+ hrs/week
New order triggers an inventory check, label print, WMS or 3PL handoff, and tracking email — without a human touching the order. Exceptions (backorder, fraud flag, wrong address) get routed to a review queue instead of slowing every good order down.
Works with: Shopify · WooCommerce · ShipStation · ShipBob · Amazon FBA · Klaviyo
2
Inventory sync & low-stock intelligence
Oversells → zero
Real-time sync between Shopify, Amazon, warehouse, and QuickBooks / NetSuite. Low-stock alerts fire before you run out (not after), with reorder suggestions based on 30-day velocity. Phantom SKUs and listing overlap get flagged weekly.
Works with: Shopify · Amazon Seller · QuickBooks · NetSuite · Cin7 · Skubana
3
Post-purchase lifecycle & winback
+15–25% LTV
Review request at delivery+7, replenishment reminder on the product's average consumption cycle, 90-day winback for lapsed buyers — all personalized with the actual product purchased, not generic "we miss you." Feeds directly into Klaviyo flows you already own.
Outputs: higher review volume · repeat-buy lift · measurable winback revenue
