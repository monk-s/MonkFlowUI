3 Highest-ROI Automations
for Financial Services

A 4-advisor firm typically loses 25–40 hours a month to client onboarding, document chase, and review-meeting prep. These three builds collapse that into minutes — and free senior advisors to actually advise.

1
Client onboarding workflow
45 min → under 5
Prospect fills a web intake → engagement letter auto-generates → goes out for e-signature → CRM record created with every field populated → kickoff meeting invited. No more duplicate data entry across Redtail, custodian, and DocuSign.
Works with: Redtail · Wealthbox · Salesforce FSC · HubSpot · DocuSign · Schwab / Fidelity forms
2
Secure document & tax-doc collection
20+ emails → 3
Clients upload W-2s, 1099s, K-1s, statements through a branded portal with deadline reminders. Files are auto-named, categorized by document type, and pushed to your tax prep software or workpaper folder. Missing-doc reminders fire without you lifting a finger.
Works with: UltraTax · Lacerte · Drake · CCH Axcess · ShareFile · Google Drive
3
Quarterly review & prep-packet automation
6–10 hrs/week back
Risk tolerance survey goes out on schedule, portfolio snapshot emails at quarter-end, review meetings auto-scheduled with a prep packet generated straight from the CRM. Senior advisors walk into every meeting with the brief done for them.
Triggers: quarter-end · life event flag · AUM threshold crossed · anniversary date
