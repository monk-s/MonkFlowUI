3 Highest-ROI Automations
for Chiropractic Practices

A 3-provider chiropractic office spends 10–12 hours a week on paper intake, manual rebooking, and chasing reviews. These three builds hand that time back and turn one-visit patients into completed care plans.

1
Digital intake & pain-assessment screening
Saves 10–15 min/new patient
Intake, pain diagram, medical history, and insurance-card photos are captured on the patient's phone before they arrive — then auto-routed into ChiroTouch or Genesis. First visit starts on time, without the clipboard ritual, and no one retypes anything.
Works with: ChiroTouch · Genesis · ChiroSpring · Jane · Zingit · Weave
2
Care-plan follow-up & rebook engine
+20–30% plan completion
After visit 1, a 4-touch education sequence drips home exercises, expected milestones, and next-phase rebook links. Patients who miss their next visit get a "we saved your slot" nudge. Phase transitions prompt the office manager to walk a patient through the next block.
Triggers: first visit complete · phase transition due · 7-day no rebook · plan 80% complete
3
Review generation & lapsed-patient reactivation
4–8 reviews/mo, 2–5 recoveries
Day-2 post-visit, happy patients get a one-tap Google review link; lukewarm ones quietly route to private feedback. At 6 weeks without a visit, a reactivation sequence ("checking in on your progress") goes out — written for chiro tone, not generic marketing spam.
Outputs: Google Maps rating lift · reactivated care · referral-ready patients
