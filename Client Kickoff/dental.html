3 Highest-ROI Automations
for Dental Practices

The average 4-provider practice loses 18+ hours a week to scheduling calls, paper intake, and reminder chase. These three builds recover that time, cut no-shows, and pay for themselves inside the first month.

1
Online scheduling + digital intake
Saves 8–12 hrs/week
Patients book themselves from your website into real chair time, tied to each provider's availability. Intake forms go out by SMS 24 hours before the visit and auto-populate into your PMS. Front desk stops being a call center.
Works with: Dentrix · Eaglesoft · Open Dental · Curve · Denticon
2
Automated reminder & confirmation sequence
Cuts no-shows 25–40%
SMS + email at 48h, 24h, and morning-of — each with a one-tap confirm, the intake link, and directions. Missed confirmations trigger a front-desk callback list instead of guesswork. Reschedules happen in the thread, not on the phone.
Triggers: booking confirmed · appointment date −2d / −1d / 0d · no-show detected
3
Hygiene recall & reactivation engine
Adds 3–8 visits/week
5-month and 6-month recall trigger automatically. Lapsed patients at 8 months get a "we miss you" sequence. Post-visit, a 2-day review request lifts Google ratings without anyone in the office remembering to ask.
Outputs: filled hygiene chairs · Google reviews · reactivated high-LTV patients
