# Discovery Call Script — 30 minutes

> **Use case:** Wealth-mgmt prospect has booked a 15-min intro or
> $1,500 audit kickoff. The first 30 minutes after intro book is
> the highest-leverage 30 minutes of the engagement. This script
> maps to the productized tiers and surfaces deal-killers fast.

---

## Pre-call (5 min before)

1. Pull the prospect's row from `outreach_leads` (or `linkedin_leads`)
2. Note: firm name, contact title, city, current CRM (if detected),
   wealth-tools detected on their site, gap they replied to
3. Open Zoom/Google Meet 2 min early — record with permission

## Opening (2 min)

> "Thanks for the time, {firstName}. Quick framing: this call is
> 30 minutes. First half I'm asking questions about your current
> new-client onboarding flow. Second half I'll show you what
> MonkFlow would do for {firmName} specifically and we'll figure out
> if there's fit. Sound good?"

Don't pitch in the first 15 minutes. **Listen.**

---

## Discovery questions (15 min)

### Context (5 min)

1. **Firm size and shape:** "How many advisors at {firmName} today?
   How many ops/admin staff? Any plans to grow this year?"
2. **AUM bracket:** "Roughly what's your AUM — under $50M, $50-100M,
   $100M+? (Determines state-registered vs SEC-registered, which
   matters for our scope.)"
3. **Custodian:** "Schwab, Fidelity, TD/Schwab, Pershing, multiple?"
4. **CRM:** "Redtail, Wealthbox, Salesforce FSC, something else? How
   long have you been on it?"
5. **Compliance:** "In-house CCO or outsourced compliance partner?"

### Current state (8 min)

6. **Walk me through a new client:** "From the moment a prospect
   says 'yes, I want to work with you' to the moment they're funded
   and showing up in your CRM dashboard — what happens?"

   *(Listen for: paper, PDFs, DocuSign volume, who does each step,
   how long, what breaks. Don't interrupt. Take notes.)*

7. **Where's the worst part?** "If you could wave a wand and fix
   one piece of that, what would it be?"

8. **What does the ops manager say?** "What's their version of the
   pain? Is it different from yours?"

9. **Have you tried fixing this before?** "Internal hack, vendor,
   consultant? What happened?"

10. **What's the cost of NOT fixing it?** "How much advisor or ops
    time goes into onboarding per new client today?" *(Surface the
    business case for THEM in their own words.)*

### Productized scope match (5 min)

11. **Volume:** "Roughly how many new clients per month/quarter?"
12. **CRM custom fields:** "Are you using custom fields heavily, or
    mostly stock Redtail/Wealthbox?" *(Custom-field complexity
    determines whether $7,500 Tier 2 fits or it has to be Tier 3.)*
13. **Custodian-of-record:** "Who handles the custodian paperwork
    today? Schwab Advisor Center, manual e-sign, paper?" *(Drives
    Tier 2 vs Tier 3 scope.)*
14. **Risk-tolerance + IPS:** "Do you want intake only, or all the
    way through to IPS draft generation?" *(Tier 2 vs Tier 3.)*
15. **Compliance review:** "Will compliance need to approve any
    document templates we generate? What's their turnaround?"

---

## What to look for (deal-killers + green-flags)

### 🟢 Green flags

- 2-10 advisors, $25M-$100M AUM (sweet spot for productized tiers)
- State-registered RIA (lower compliance overhead than SEC-registered)
- Already on Redtail or Wealthbox (CRMs we've built for / understand)
- 1-3 new clients per month (volume matches productized scope)
- "Yes, we know it's broken; we just haven't fixed it" (intent-ready)
- Founding partner is on the call (decision-maker access)

### 🔴 Deal-killers (be honest, decline politely)

- 100+ advisors or $1B+ AUM — too big for productized; offer custom
  engagement quote later instead
- SEC-registered with vendor-questionnaire process — not Tier 1/2/3
  ready; flag for Q4 if compliance posture matures
- Custodian we don't know (e.g., niche international) — scope risk
- "We'll know it when we see it" without a current pain — not buyer-ready
- Hybrid broker-dealer (different paperwork regime) — not the ICP
- No CRM today and not willing to start with one — too big a lift

If any of these surface, **say so politely on the call.** "I don't
think MonkFlow is the right fit because [X]. Happy to point you to
[alternative if you have one]." This builds reputation — the
prospect tells their network.

---

## Pitch (8 min)

After the discovery, transition with: *"Based on what you just told
me, here's what MonkFlow would do for {firmName} — and I'll be
honest about what we wouldn't."*

### What we'd do (3 min)

Tie back to THEIR pain in THEIR words:
- "You said the worst part is [X]. We'd build [specific thing]."
- "You said the ops manager spends [Y] minutes per onboarding on
  [Z]. We've done that for TFS — got it from 45 minutes to under 5."
- "Your CRM is {their CRM}. We've integrated with that before /
  building it would take an extra [N] days vs Redtail."

### Tier recommendation (2 min)

Pick ONE tier explicitly, with the reason:
- "I'd recommend Tier 2 (Intake Pro, $7,500). You don't need IPS
  drafting because your CIO does that already; intake + CRM sync is
  the highest-leverage win." OR
- "I'd recommend Tier 3 (Onboarding System, $14,500) because you
  said the IPS drafting is a 90-min-per-client time sink. Tier 2
  doesn't include that; Tier 3 does."

Make the recommendation specific. Don't make them choose.

### What we wouldn't do (2 min)

Disqualify yourself from things you don't do or don't recommend:
- "We're not your CRM provider. If you want to switch CRMs, we're
  the wrong vendor for that."
- "We don't build trading platforms. If your bottleneck is trade
  execution, that's a different vendor."
- "We don't do compliance consulting. If your CCO needs to review
  the templates we generate, that's their work, not ours."

### Pricing & next step (1 min)

> "Pricing is on monkflow.io/#wealth-intake — public, fixed-fee.
> For {firmName}, the {tier} at ${price} fits. {N}-day delivery from
> kickoff.
>
> Two ways to move forward:
>
> 1. **Audit first** ($1,500, 7-day delivery, refundable against the
>    project). I do a 90-min Loom walkthrough of your current flow and
>    write a report on the 3 highest-ROI fixes. If you book the
>    project after, the audit cost is credited.
>
> 2. **Skip the audit** — we go straight to project quote and signed
>    engagement letter.
>
> Which fits better for {firmName}?"

## Close (5 min)

### If they choose Audit:

- Send Stripe invoice for $1,500 within 1 hour of call ending
- Schedule audit Loom delivery 7 days out
- Calendar block YOUR time for the audit work in next 2 days
- Send DPA draft for signature pre-audit

### If they choose Project:

- Send engagement letter draft within 24 hours
- Send DPA draft simultaneously
- Schedule kickoff call once both signed
- Stripe invoice on signed letter (50% upfront, 50% on go-live)

### If they want to "think about it":

- Confirm in writing within 24 hours: meeting summary, recommended
  tier, pricing, next-step options, decision deadline (10 business
  days). No "let me know whenever."
- Add to your personal CRM with deadline. Follow up at day 5 and
  day 10. After that, mark as closed.

---

## After the call (15 min)

1. Write meeting summary email within 1 hour. Bullet list of what
   they said + what you proposed + next steps + price.
2. Update prospect record (admin UI): triage status, recommended
   tier, decision deadline.
3. If they were a green-flag fit, add to YOUR personal CRM (not
   MonkFlow's outreach tables) for active deal tracking.
4. Schedule the follow-up tasks per "If they want to think about
   it" above.
