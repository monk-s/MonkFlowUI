## What to bring to the alliance call

1. The 1-page deck (printed or PDF on screen)
2. The TFS case study page (monkflow.io/case-studies/team-financial-strategies — once published)
3. The 1-pager intake-forms PDF (`docs/wealth-mgmt-pivot/01-intake-forms-1pager.md` rendered)
4. The reply-handling SOP (so they know you'll handle their referrals
   responsively)
5. A list of 2-3 prospect firms YOU know in their network — to give
   them a "here's how I'd use this" example before they're committing

---

## What to ask on the call

1. **Their book of business:** how many active clients fit MonkFlow's
   ICP (state-registered RIA, $25M-$100M AUM)?
2. **Their referral cadence:** how often do they actively refer
   vendors today, and to whom?
3. **What kills their referrals:** what makes them HESITATE to
   refer a vendor? (Compliance posture? Bad past experience?
   Vendor not responsive?)
4. **Their preferred mechanism:** warm email intro? Slack channel?
   Joint dinner? Whatever they say YES to is the channel.

---

## After the alliance is signed

Make it impossible to forget MonkFlow:

1. Send them a stocked "referral kit" — 1-page deck (PDF), TFS case
   study link, the intake-forms 1-pager, your direct contact info,
   their referral fee terms.
2. Send a quarterly check-in: any RIAs come across your desk last
   quarter where MonkFlow could fit? (Not pushy. Just present.)
3. When a referred client closes, pay the fee within 30 days, send
   a personal thank-you, and post a quick LinkedIn shoutout to the
   alliance partner. Reciprocity compounds.
